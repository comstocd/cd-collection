Shelf positions follow the 4 × 6 cubby plan: R1 is the top row, C1 the left column. Within a section, solo artists file by surname and bands by first word, ignoring "The"; the Beatles and the first Grateful Dead cubby run in release order. Label, catalogue number, barcode and pressing date come from the MusicBrainz release your drive matched when the disc was ripped in January 2023, or from the spine photos for discs not ripped (including every SACD, which a computer drive can't read). The pressing year is that edition's release, not the album's original year. Entries marked CHECK are partly unreadable spines.
